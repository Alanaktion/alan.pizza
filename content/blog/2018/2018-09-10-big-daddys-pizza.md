Today I tried the Big Daddy's medium pepperoni pizza. It's pretty cheap, but pretty good! Their crust is always my favorite part of their pizzas. For the price, it's a really good crust. They also typically add a *lot* of cheese, which is always nice. This one was just about perfect! At least as close as you can get to perfection for about $4.
