Domino's has a new stuffed crust pizza option, and as a fan of both Domino's and stuffed crust pizzas I had to try it!

Every stuffed crust pizza is surprisingly very different, and this one is no exception. While I've always felt the Pizza Hut stuffed crust is the default experience, this is a different but excellent pizza. Some pizzas don't do well with the stuffed crust addition, especially when the crust itself is mediocre, but this is a great new take on the traditional Domino's crust and I love it.

It's really, really good. An extra $4 is worth it if you love stuffed crust.
