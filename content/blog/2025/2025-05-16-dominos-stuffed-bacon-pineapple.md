The last time I tried the Domino's stuffed crust I kept it fairly simple with pepperoni and green peppers. This time I tried bacon and pineapple, a combination that sometimes works great and sometimes is only okay. This time it was good but not amazing.

I definitely prefer the previous Domino's stuffed crust I had over this one, but it was still a good pizza. Solid 7/10. I still think pineapple works best with their pan crust though.
