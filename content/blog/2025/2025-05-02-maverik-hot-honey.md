Following up on [last week's post](/2025/04/21/maverik-pepperoni/), I tried another option from Maverik.

This is the Hot Honey Double Pepperoni, a slight addition to their regular pepperoni. It's sweet and delicious! Definitely recommend trying it, as it's a fun unique twist on a typical pizza. I'm not sure if I'd consistently pick it over a regular pepperoni slice, but it's good.
