On the Santa Monica Pier in California, there's a little pizza restaurant that's actually really good! For around $9, you can get an excellent personal pizza, with very high quality toppings and a pretty decent crust.

My sister tried the LA Dog from the restaurant next door and it looked amazing, so if you're at the pier, you should maybe give both a try.
