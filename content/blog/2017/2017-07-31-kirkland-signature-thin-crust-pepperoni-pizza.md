This is one of the better Costco pizzas. The pepperoni is fantastic, and the crust is fairly decent. Not much to say, there's not much to it, but it's not bad! I'd definitely go for a [California Pizza Kitchen Signature Pepperoni](2017-01-10-california-pizza-kitchen-signature-pepperoni.md) as a similar pizza if it were an option, but these were significantly cheaper.
