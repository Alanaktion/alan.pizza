I've had some excellent Margherita pizzas before, but getting a frozen one for $6 just doesn't quite work out as well. There's so little to the ingredients on a Margherita that it's quite hard to make a really good one. This one's not *bad*, but it's not all that great either. I'd greatly prefer one of the other California Pizza Kitchen frozen pizzas.

★★☆☆☆
