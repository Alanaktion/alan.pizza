I've always loved Pizza Pie Cafe. It's a buffet with a wide variety of pizzas, including several desserts. They also have pasta and salad, but why would you want that when there's pizza?

When we got there today, they were just taking out fresh cheese sticks and Hillbilly (3-meat) pizza, both of which are amazing. The cheese sticks were *perfect*, but the pepperoni pizza and garlic knots were a bit too doughy this time.

I've found that the quality of the pizza varies significantly at PPC, likely depending on who's making it that day, but it's usually pretty good. For a buffet, the price isn't too bad, especially if you're subscribed to their SMS coupons, which are sent every Monday and Wednesday, and good for two days.

★★★★☆
