Our IT department had a pizza party at work today and things got a little crazy. I work for a freeze-dried food company, [Thrive Life](https://alan.thrivelife.com/shop), which makes a wide variety of delicious freeze-dried foods. Our chairman, Jason Budge, joined us and made a very unique pizza. This is a grocery store pre-baked crust with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese, black olives, and… THRIVE freeze-dried [Greek Yogurt Bites](http://alan.thrivelife.com/greek-yogurt.html) and [Fuji Apple Slices](http://alan.thrivelife.com/fuji-apple-slices-freeze-dried.html) . Certainly not a typical pizza, but Thrive Life isn't a typical food company.

I didn't try the pizza myself, but I was told the part with the Fuji Apple Slices was actually decent. The Greek Yogurt Bites didn't quite work with the cheese and tomato sauce, but are apparently really good toasted on their own, so I'll definitely be trying that as a snack in the future.
