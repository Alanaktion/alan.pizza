Quite possibly my favorite frozen pizza is the California Pizza Kitchen Signature Pepperoni. They're a bit pricey as far as frozen pizzas go, but the ingredients are very good quality. The combination of cheeses is really good, and the crust has the perfect consistency for a thin pizza.

★★★★☆
