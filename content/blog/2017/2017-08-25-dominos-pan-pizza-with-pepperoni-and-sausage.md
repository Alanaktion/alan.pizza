I think this was my first time trying th Domino's pan crust. It's not bad, but I think I prefer their hand-tossed crust, since this isn't quite as good a Pizza Hut's pan crust. The pepperoni and Italian sausage combination is always a good choice, but overall this pizza is just a bit lacking. Still a good Friday evening treat though!
