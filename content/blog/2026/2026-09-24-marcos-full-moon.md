All menu-priced items are 50% off today at Marco's, so we tried some specialty pizzas.

![Photo of a margherita pizza, with lots of cheese, tomato slices, and basil, in a pizza box.](/blog/2026/img/IMG_20260924_181642.jpeg)

The Margherita Magnifico is the first one we tried. It's pretty good, though far from the best margherita pizza I've had. The cheese was quite thick and the crust was very thin, and felt like overall the wrong ratio of things for a margherita, but it's still a good pizza.

![Photo of a pizza in a box, with multiple kinds and sizes of pepperoni.](/blog/2026/img/IMG_20260924_181702.jpeg)

Next is the Pepperoni Magnifico, which combines their standard pepperoni and Old World Pepperoni, with Romano and Parmesan cheeses added. It's a solid pizza, though it is quite greasy. Definitely a step up from a standard pepperoni, and while I don't think I'd typically pay the extra over their discounted 1-topping option, at 50% off it's great.

![Photo of cheese bread on parchment paper in a cardboard box, with upside-down sauce cups.](/blog/2026/img/IMG_20260924_181624.jpeg)

Last is the CheezyBread. It's okay. I feel like there's a wide range of cheese breadsticks at pizza places, and most of them are just… fine. It's not *bad* but it's just some thick pizza dough with a little bit of cheese on top. They include ranch and pizza sauce with the bread, so yeah.

Overall, while Marco's is typically more expensive than I'd consider "worth it", at 50% off for all menu-priced items, it's a great deal on great pizza.
