This one's a little different. It's a potato-based thin crust, cheese pizza from Tombstone. I like trying the more unique frozen pizzas, 'cause you never know what you'll really get.

This pizza's okay. I like that it's interesting and new, and it's worth trying once, but I don't expect I'll get another one. They have two other topping options on this same crust, and it's *fine* but I'd rather just have something else :P
