Domino's has a new personal size pizza! It's called The Domino, presumably 'cause it's kinda shaped a bit like a domino.

It's *very* good. Basically a much higher quality version of the Little Caesar's deep dish crust pizza, a bit smaller but much better. It's light and flaky despite the thick crust, and the overall balance is great.

I would definitely recommend giving it a try!

![A photo of a pizza with cheese, sausage, and chicken, on a black table. A cute white-fur cat is sitting on the table next to the pizza.](/blog/2026/img/IMG_20261016_2.jpg)

I also got some other pizzas, since the Domino's Mix & Match deal is kind of just too good to pass up if I'm going there already anyway. This was just a basic sausage and chicken pizza, simple but good.
