Decided to try Marco's again, the last time was [quite a while ago](/2018/09/01/marcos-pizza/). This time I went for the Deluxe pizza, with pepperoni, sausage, and green peppers. It's a pretty solid pizza, definitely something I'd recommend, but it's pretty pricey. Even with a $3 discount, my order was ~$15 after tax for a single medium pizza, which is harder to justify when the $6.99 Domino's mix-and-match deal still exists.

Great pizza though, with a good crust, good toppings, good cheese, and a lot of grease.
